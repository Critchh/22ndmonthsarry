Open Me

To the girl of my dreams,

Good afternoon to you my love, I hope you slept good today; I can’t wait to see your face in person again, to hold you and kiss your soft skin, to bask in the essence of your warmth and to enjoy being in your presence once again; I miss everything, from your smell, to our coffees in the morning, afternoon, and even at night, to our never-ending food runs, our small dates, our walking Hera time, our infinite quest to find something new to watch or do, it will always be etched in my soul how happy those times when I’m with you are.

You’re everything I’d ever want, my darling; My partner in life and in crime, in sadness and in happiness, in excitement and in boredom, you are the only one I want, even if my heart stops beating. I know we have been going through some rough patches, most definitely because of my doing as well, I just want you to know that I love you so much and if I could be by your side at every given second, minute, hour, days, weeks, and years, I will take it even if it costs me my all, even if it costs me my past, my future, and my present, I will take it. There is absolutely nothing that can amount to my love for you and I want you to know that, and feel that, it’s so sweet, knowing that you love me, to have such a wonderful woman in my arms and in my heart, someone that is willing to give her all and more for me. I thank you, I promise that if this world was mine, I would also conquer other planets for you and it will still be a tad bit too short to amount to my love for you.

I wish to hear the sound of the waves collide with you by my side once again. I know we share the same love for the ocean and I feel that in a way it connects us; our love is as vast as it is and our bond to be as deep as it is and no one would ever know this but us. I wish to look you at this moment in your eyes with the thickest tension mankind has ever seen, to melt you with my gaze and to lay with you without space.

A most wonderful 22nd to us my love, I hope that we can do the most in our day. I will be taking my day offs for Thursday and Friday and I might call in for Wednesday to spend more time with you on our Monthsary. Just know that I always got your back, you will always have me, now and forever, wherever you go that’s where I’ll follow .



From a thousand miles away, yet closest in my heart,

Ian
